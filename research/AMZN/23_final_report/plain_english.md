# Amazon.com, Inc. (AMZN) — In Plain English

## In Plain English

### What the company does
Amazon.com, Inc. operates the world's largest online shopping marketplace, delivers enterprise cloud computing and artificial intelligence infrastructure through AWS, runs digital streaming services (Prime Video), and manages a massive logistics network.

### Why investors may like it
* **Cloud computing powerhouse**: AWS is the global cloud leader, generating high-margin enterprise recurring cash flow.
* **Surging profitability**: Fulfillment regionalization and high-margin advertising are driving record operating profit margins.
* **Prime flywheel**: Hundreds of millions of Prime members provide high customer retention and recurring subscription spend.

### Why investors may avoid it
* **Heavy AI capital spending**: Massive capex investments into AI chips, servers, and data centers could compress near-term free cash flow.
* **Antitrust regulatory pressure**: Regulators in the US and Europe continue investigating retail marketplace practices and seller fees.

### What is attractive
* Top-tier financial safety with low debt and enormous cash reserves (Altman Z-Score: 5.82, Safe Zone).
* Dependable profits and high return on every dollar invested into the business (ROIC: 17.2%).

### What is expensive

### What is risky

### What is improving
* Retail operational efficiencies and digital advertising revenue are driving rapid operating income margin expansion.
